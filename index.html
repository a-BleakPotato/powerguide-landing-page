Common Questions with Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Power Guide, its features, and how it can help you stay prepared during power interruptions.

What is PowerGuide and how does it work?

PowerGuide helps you prepare for power interruptions with outage reports, hazard alerts, battery monitoring, and appliance safety timers.

How can I report a power outage in my area?

Open Outage Reporting, add your location and details, then submit. You can track the progress of your report afterwards.

Can I see power outages and hazards near my location?

Yes. The map shows community-reported outages and hazards close to you.

How can I monitor my devices’ battery levels?

Add your devices to see their battery levels in one place and get a warning when one runs low.

What are appliance safety timers and how do they work?

A timer reminds you to switch off or unplug an appliance after a set time, so nothing is left running when the power returns.

How can I find nearby charging stations?

Use the charging station finder to see places near you where you can charge your devices.

Will I receive notifications about outages and hazards in my area?

Yes. Turn on notifications to get alerts about outages and hazards where you are.

Do I need to set my location to use PowerGuide?

Setting your location gives the most relevant results, but you can still browse general information without it.
